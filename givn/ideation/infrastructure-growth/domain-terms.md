## Candidate domain sentences

- Adding a photo application to the family datacenter must not require the family to adopt a cloud application.
- After an 18:00 failure, a photo recovery point from 02:00 that day is sixteen hours old.
- The photo application's delegated recovery-point target is 24 hours and imposes no strict recovery-duration deadline.
- An application-consistent backup contains the photo application's required coherent state, not merely a directory named backup.
- Photo originals and required application metadata are persistent application state; their local persistence alone does not protect against losing the disk.
- The photo application's guest flow permits ordinary internet reachability while the app still decides access to the selected album.

## Evidence distinctions and limitations

Keep local persistence, a provider/local snapshot, external firewall-policy backup, and application-state backup distinct. Local persistence does not survive loss of its own storage. Provider-snapshot survival and application consistency are not established by the inspected plan; do not assert that every provider snapshot either does or does not survive a particular failure. Firewall-policy JSON recovery is not photo-application recovery. The existing restic plan demonstrates a proposal for file-path capture, not a tested application-consistent restore.

The scout's suggested contrast of family datacenter versus an experimentation-only home lab was not adopted: the user uses both for the same family-serving environment. Its suggested definition making "retained state" inherently off-host was also not adopted: state retention and backup-copy survival are separate properties. Definitions above resolve those wording risks mechanically without changing user goals or promoting terms. No aggregate is introduced.

The owner's working environment is ready only when usual work can resume with required files/state and permitted identity access, as accepted in Q15. Declarative configuration does not recreate unavailable working data or authorize arbitrary credential duplication. Its VPN software/configuration belongs to selected application needs rather than a universal base requirement.
